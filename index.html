01. about

I build software and AI products that hold up in real environments.

I'm a Software Development Engineer II at Amazon, where I build high-availability AWS applications, backend services, data workflows, and operational tooling for large-scale investigation and response systems. The domain is security; my core discipline is software engineering: performance, reliability, observability, automation, and systems that teams can operate with confidence.

My current focus is AI engineering. I build AI-powered products and agentic workflows, alongside the full-stack software that makes them useful. EchoFocus is my clearest example right now: a live full-stack AI product that turns text, files, photos, and live recordings into an audio-first experience with synchronized transcripts, AI summaries, and searchable history. I am also building a reusable agentic automation platform that combines LLM-backed reasoning, deterministic workflows, and integrations with external tools and services.

My research and writing have used security as a practical domain for AI, automation, data processing, and distributed systems. I am pursuing an MS in Computer Science at UT Austin with a focus on Machine Learning and AI, and I am most interested in the engineering work required to make intelligent software reliable, observable, and useful in production.
